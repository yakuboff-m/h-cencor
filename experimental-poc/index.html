Why 60 FPS Overlay ≠ 60 FPS Censorship

The Latency Trap: An overlay canvas rendering at 60 FPS gives a deceptive appearance of smoothness. If neural inference executes every 100ms (10 Hz), naive sample-and-hold causes the censor box to lag 12–35px behind rapid camera pans, leaving anatomical targets fully exposed for 3–6 frames. Coupling requestVideoFrameCallback with velocity vector extrapolation completely eliminates this spatial lag.
