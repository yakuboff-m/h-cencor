Why 60 FPS Overlay ≠ 60 FPS Censorship

The Latency Illusion: An extension can draw an HTML5 canvas overlay at 60 FPS, but if the neural detector executes every 100ms (10 Hz), the censorship box lags 100ms behind fast-moving video subjects. During rapid camera cuts or pans, sensitive anatomy is exposed for 3–6 frames before the detector catches up unless spatial tracking or proactive motion vectors are applied.
